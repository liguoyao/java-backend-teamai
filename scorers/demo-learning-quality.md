Evaluate whether the completed work in this session supports a Learning that the demo team can reuse. Use the actual actions and outcomes shown in the supplied evidence. If the session drafts a Learning, assess its material claims and instructions as written. Otherwise assess the strongest concrete lesson demonstrated by the work.

- Evidence: Identify a specific observation and its corroborating tool result, test, inspected source, or explicit user confirmation. A request, plan, or uncorroborated completion claim is insufficient.
- Action: State which repeatable step or decision a teammate could apply and what it would accomplish.
- Scope: Identify the triggering conditions and the affected component, workflow, or environment. Keep the claim within what the evidence supports.

Choose insufficient_evidence when there is no substantiated transferable lesson, including when the input contains only the initial request. Choose needs_revision when the evidence supports a lesson but a teammate lacks a usable action or enough scope to apply it correctly. Choose ready_to_share only when all three criteria are met. Cite the specific evidence and identify any missing criterion in your reasoning. Avoid reproducing credentials or unrelated private details.
